## 1. Brand Essence & Creative Philosophy
The Ninth Spice represents the pinnacle of Indian single-origin spice harvesting. Unlike industrial conglomerates that pulverize spices at high-RPM scorching heats or online grocers that treat spices as commoditized ingredients, The Ninth Spice approaches spices as cultural terroir—slow stone-ground at ambient temperatures, preserving delicate volatile aromatic oils and natural, intense culinary pigment.

The visual identity must reflect **editorial luxury, cultural grounding, human craftsmanship, and uncompromising transparency**.

- **Warm Ivory (`#F8F4EB`)** is the primary background across all pages, creating an authentic parchment feel without sterile digital whites.

- **Deep Chocolate (`#24140F`)** anchors the header wordmark, primary typography, and the authoritative footer.

## 3. Typography Architecture

### 3.1 Font Families
- **Editorial Display:** `Cormorant Garamond`, `Playfair Display`, `Cinzel` (Google Fonts)
  - Character: Dignified, historic, refined serifs with graceful stroke contrast.
  - Usage: Brand wordmarks, major section headlines, product titles, philosophical quotes.
- **Body & Technical Specs:** `DM Sans`, `Manrope`, `Inter` (Google Fonts)
  - Character: Clean, geometric, legible at micro-sizes with generous apertures.
  - Usage: Narrative paragraphs, nutrition breakdowns, pack sizes, checkout forms.
- **Monospace Metadata:** `ui-monospace`, `SFMono-Regular`, `Menlo`
  - Usage: Batch numbers, PIN code entry, harvest years, FSSAI verification codes.

## 4. Layout, Geometry & Whitespace

### 4.1 Goodbye to AI Web-Builder Archetypes:
- **Corner Radii:** Replaced bulbous `rounded-3xl` (24px–32px) with sophisticated, architectural radii (`rounded-xl` [12px] and `rounded-2xl` [16px]).
- **Borders:** Subtle, warm borders (`border-[#E9DDC9]`) that establish clear spatial boundaries without heavy outlines.
- **Shadows:** Natural, diffuse elevation (`shadow-xs` and `shadow-sm`) instead of artificial colored drop-shadows.
- **Intentional Asymmetry:** Staggered editorial columns, overlapping packaging imagery, and generous breathing room that guides the eye naturally.
